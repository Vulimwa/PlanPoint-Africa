Who We Are

PlanPoint Africa is a geospatial and planning firm based in Kenya. We help public and private sector clients make sense of land and place through spatial data, mapping and planning services.

Our work brings together technical analysis and local context to support practical, informed decisions about land, infrastructure and development.

What We Do

We provide GIS and geospatial mapping, spatial data analysis, land-use planning and physical planning support. Our services help clients understand site conditions, assess development options and communicate plans clearly.

Each assignment is shaped around its objectives, location and relevant planning context, with a focus on useful information and considered outcomes.
