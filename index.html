About us

PlanPoint Africa is a Kenyan geospatial and planning firm supporting informed decisions about land and development. We combine spatial data, mapping and planning expertise to help clients understand places, evaluate options and plan for sustainable growth.

Our Services

Geospatial mapping and GIS

We organise, analyse and present spatial data to reveal patterns and support clear, evidence-based decisions. GIS maps can help clients understand land, infrastructure, services and development opportunities.

Physical and land-use planning

We support land-use planning and physical planning through site analysis, spatial plans and development guidance tailored to local context and applicable planning requirements.

Land and development advisory

We help clients assess land and development considerations, bringing together site information, planning context and practical spatial analysis to inform next steps.

Spatial data and mapping

From data capture and map production to spatial visualisation, we make location-based information easier to interpret, communicate and use.
